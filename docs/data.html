Data

Our dataset consists of detector waveforms collected by the Majorana Demonstrator experiment. Each waveform represents charge collection behavior in high-purity germanium detectors.

  • Waveforms stored in HDF5 files
  • Fixed-length time-series samples
  • Event-level PSD labels
  • Energy labels for regression analysis

Engineered Features

tp0

Initial timing reference of the waveform used to anchor pulse measurements.

ED

Energy duration measuring how long the pulse remains above threshold.

Half-Width Peak (HWP)

Measures the temporal width of the waveform peak, indicating how localized the energy deposition is.

LQ80

Captures waveform charge behavior at 80% amplitude. Useful for distinguishing single-site from multi-site events.

PPR

Peak-to-Peak Ratio capturing amplitude structure within the waveform.

SCA

Shape comparison attribute summarizing waveform morphology.

ND80

Drift-related timing feature that reflects charge transport behavior within the detector.

current_skewness

Skewness of the current signal distribution, capturing asymmetry in pulse shape.

tail_slope_no_pz

Tail slope measurement without pole-zero correction applied.

spectral_centroid_power

Frequency-domain feature summarizing how signal energy is distributed across frequencies.

current_kurtosis

Kurtosis of the current waveform distribution, measuring peakedness.

tail_slope

Slope of the waveform tail region following the main pulse.

total_power

Total integrated signal power derived from frequency analysis.

time_to_main_peak

Time taken for the waveform to reach its primary peak.

time_to_peak

Overall time required for the waveform to reach maximum amplitude.

current_width

Temporal width of the current pulse.

tdrift10

Drift time measured at 10% of the waveform amplitude.

tdrift50

Drift time measured at 50% of the waveform amplitude.

tdrift99

Drift time measured at 99% of the waveform amplitude.

tfr

Time-to-front ratio capturing pulse rise characteristics.

peak_count

Number of significant peaks detected in the waveform.

gbn

Gradient-based noise metric reflecting signal fluctuations.

bpr

Baseline power ratio feature derived from waveform baseline behavior.

AvsE

Amplitude versus Energy feature commonly used for pulse shape discrimination.

GradAreaRatio

Ratio of gradient area capturing waveform shape characteristics.

GradWidthMain

Gradient-based width measurement of the main pulse region.

HFER

High-Frequency Energy Ratio derived from spectral components of the waveform.
